# Vernac

Localize your entire App Store Connect listing — descriptions, keywords, promotional text, screenshots, and Game Center — into every language, then push it straight to your editable draft. A native macOS app for App Store developers, by an indie developer.

Vernac reads your app's localizable metadata through the App Store Connect API, lets you translate and edit every field side‑by‑side with your source language (on‑device with Apple's Translation framework, or with your own AI assistant), shows you an exact diff of what will change, and writes **only** to your editable draft. It never submits your app for you.
